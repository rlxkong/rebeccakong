Autonomous Underwater Vehicle

ArduinoMATLAB

Multi-sensor circuitry for an autonomous underwater vehicle supporting ocean-sampling and environmental monitoring.

Designed and integrated multi-sensor circuitry for an autonomous underwater vehicle supporting ocean-sampling and environmental monitoring. Interfaced six sensor systems, including GPS, IMU, pressure, temperature, motor-current, and magnetometer sensors, with Arduino for onboard data acquisition. Developed MATLAB code to analyze sensor data for water-current mapping and magnetic-object detection.
